## Symptoms

Broker connect failed: No NIFTY PE strike 23500 for expiry 2026-06-23 in instrument master

## Business impact

KAVACH and SARANSH could not load virtual positions; /register and /positions broken

## Technical impact

create_broker() raised LookupError during ShadowBroker._reload_positions_from_fixture

## Trading impact

ATO module did not start; no mock order flow

## Root cause

uat/deployed_positions/positions.json retained past weekly expiry; instrument master drops expired series

## Contributing factors

- Fixture captured 2026-06-13 for 23 Jun expiry
- No auto-roll on weekly expiry boundary

## Investigation

logs_uat/.../kavach/errors/all_errors.log 10:46 IST; incident CSV kavach::log_error

## Fix implemented

resolve_fixture_trading_expiry() rolls to nearest valid weekly; ShadowBroker clears virtual ledger on roll

## Why this fix works

UAT book remains loadable without manual fixture edit every expiry day; stale virtual fills cannot corrupt qty

## Regression risk

New weekly expiry without strikes in master may still fail — operator should refresh screenshot
